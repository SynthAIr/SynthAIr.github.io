# Research Outputs

The research and development in the SynthAIr project are disseminated through peer-reviewed publications, public deliverables, and open-source repositories. This section provides a comprehensive overview of our contributions to the field of AI-driven air traffic management.

**Note:** *The SynthAIr project has concluded. All public deliverables have been submitted to the SESAR 3 Joint Undertaking and are being progressively published on CORDIS and Zenodo as they receive final approval. Scientific publications from the project are available in open access via the links below.*
